Both the initial onset of the pulse and specific low acceleration disturbances (< 0.5g) heavily influence the behaviour at the start of the pulse. For that reason, it is in practice not possible to identify the actual start of the pulse. Acceleration levels higher than 1g however are unmistakably a direct result of the pulse on the sled.
